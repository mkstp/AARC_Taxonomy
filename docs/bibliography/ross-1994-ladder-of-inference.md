## Project Relevance

Ross's chapter in the Fifth Discipline Fieldbook is the widely-recognized practitioner formulation of the Ladder of Inference, a concept originating in Argyris (1982). The chapter explicitly credits Argyris and presents the ladder as a graphical tool for surfacing untested inferences in organizational dialogue. Cited alongside Argyris (1982) in the position paper for the inference overshoot signal in the Clarity component of the AARC schema. Note: Senge did not author this chapter and did not introduce the concept; "Senge (1994)" as a citation for the Ladder of Inference is bibliographically imprecise.
