# Thompson, K., Chaturvedi, A., & Asher, N. (2025). Does discourse structure help action prediction? A look at correction triangles.

## Citation

Thompson, K., Chaturvedi, A., & Asher, N. (2025). Does discourse structure help action prediction? A look at correction triangles. *Proceedings of the 16th International Conference on Computational Semantics (IWCS)* (pp. 166–174).

## Abstract

An understanding of natural language corrections is essential for artificial agents that are meant to collaborate and converse with humans. We present some preliminary experiments using language-to-action models investigating whether discourse structure, in particular Correction relations, improves the action prediction capabilities of language-to-action models for simple block world tasks. We focus on scenarios in which a model must correct a previous action, and present a corpus of synthetic dialogues to help explain model performance.

## Project Relevance

This paper investigates whether discourse-level structural features improve prediction of corrective speech acts in dialogue, with direct relevance to the contradiction and correction detection components of the FPO submodel. Its treatment of correction events as discourse-structural phenomena — not merely surface-level contradiction — bears on how correction events are labeled and resolved in the trajectory generation pipeline, and on the design of the annotation schema's treatment of contradiction as a friction signal.
