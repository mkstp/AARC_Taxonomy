## Project Relevance

Argyris is the originator of the Ladder of Inference — the model by which an agent ascends from observable data through added meaning, assumption, and conclusion to action, without verification at intermediate steps. The concept grounds the inference overshoot signal in the Clarity component of the AARC schema: a speaker who treats an unverified inferential conclusion as a settled premise has climbed the ladder without a rung. The primary scholarly attribution for this concept belongs here; Ross (1994) in Senge et al. is the widely-cited practitioner formulation but acknowledges Argyris as the source.
