## Project Relevance

Bradley and Terry establish the probabilistic model for pairwise comparisons in which items are assigned latent utility scores and the probability that item A is preferred to item B is a function of the difference in those scores. This is the formal statistical foundation on which reward models trained from human preference pairs are constructed: the cross-entropy loss used in RLHF reward model training is a direct application of the Bradley-Terry likelihood.
