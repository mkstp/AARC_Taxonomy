## Project Relevance

Deutsch establishes that authentic cooperative engagement in conflict presupposes parties' awareness that they are neither helpless nor powerless — making perceived power parity a precondition, not merely a resource, for constructive negotiation. The book is a foundational text in conflict resolution theory and is cited here for its grounding of Agency as a structural precondition: where a party perceives itself as powerless, neither clarification nor persuasion is sufficient to restore productive engagement.
