## Project Relevance

Thibaut and Walker establish empirically that disputants' acceptance of and willingness to engage with dispute resolution procedures is determined primarily by their perception of process control — the belief that they can meaningfully present their case and influence the procedure — independent of outcome favorability. This finding grounds AARC's Agency component as a precondition: a party who perceives themselves as excluded from or unable to influence the process will not engage constructively regardless of the content of any proposed resolution. Procedural justice is cited in the position paper as the primary scholarly anchor for Agency as a precondition, replacing the earlier citation to Fisher and Ury's BATNA concept, which grounded Agency instrumentally rather than as a structural condition for engagement. *Verify: Lawrence Erlbaum publication details before submission.*
