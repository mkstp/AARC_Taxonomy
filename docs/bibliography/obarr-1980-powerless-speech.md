## Project Relevance

O'Barr and Atkins coin the term "powerless speech" through courtroom testimony analysis, demonstrating that the hedging, qualifiers, and disfluencies Lakoff (1975) identified correlate with low institutional status and power rather than gender per se. This is the primary source for the paper's use of "powerless speech patterns (hedging, qualifiers, short replies following long turns)" as an observable Agency deficit signal: these features mark a party whose capacity to meaningfully influence the interaction is structurally constrained, which is the condition AARC's Agency component targets.
