## Project Relevance

Bradbury and Fincham synthesize experimental, clinical, and longitudinal evidence showing that dissatisfied partners systematically interpret counterpart behavior through prior negative relational history — attributing current actions to stable, global, and intentional causes. This is the primary empirical anchor for the paper's use of "attributional lensing" as a Clarity deficit signal: the mechanism by which accumulated grievance distorts causal inference about a counterpart's current intent, producing an intent-action-effect gap that content-level correction cannot resolve without first interrupting the attributional pattern.
