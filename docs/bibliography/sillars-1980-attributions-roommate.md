## Project Relevance

Sillars establishes the communication-theoretic grounding for attributional lensing: communicative decisions in conflict are governed by attributions of intent, causality, and stability, and biases along these dimensions drive avoidant or distributive rather than integrative strategy. Cited alongside Bradbury and Fincham (1990) to support the operationalization of attributional lensing as a turn-level Clarity deficit. Sillars extends the construct beyond the marital dyad to interpersonal conflict more generally, broadening the applicability of the citation to the paper's conflict corpus.
